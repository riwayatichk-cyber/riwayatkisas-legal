6. Reporting a copyright concern

If you believe that content in the App infringes your copyright or another right, send a notice to userconplay@gmail.com including:

  1. Your name and how to contact you.
  2. The work you own and, if possible, proof of your rights (for example the original publication).
  3. The content in the App you are concerned about — for example the story’s title and part number — so we can find it.
  4. A statement that you believe in good faith that the use is not authorised, and that the information in your notice is accurate and that you are the rights holder or authorised to act for them.

We review every notice, reply within 14 days, and remove or correct content that we find infringing in the next update of the App. If your content was removed by mistake, you can send us a counter-notice with the same details and we will reconsider.
