1. Summary

  • No account. The App has no sign-in and never asks for your name, e-mail address or phone number.
  • Your personal spaces stay on your device. Reading progress, favourites and quotes, diary notes and their photos, your daily schedule, habits, prayer log, period and pregnancy records, wardrobe and game progress are stored only in the App’s private storage on your phone. We run no servers that receive them.
  • Health information never leaves your device. Period and pregnancy records are not sent to us or to any service, are never used for ads or analytics, and are excluded from Android cloud backup.
  • What reaches Google: crash reports, usage statistics, a push-notification token, an app-integrity check, remote-configuration requests, and the device’s Advertising ID used for ads and analytics.
  • Your controls: the ad consent settings inside the App, notification switches, section locks, Android permission settings, and resetting or deleting your Advertising ID.

2. How the App works

The App is an offline library of Arabic novels and stories, together with personal everyday spaces — a diary, a daily schedule, habits, prayer times and adhkar, period tracking, pregnancy tracking, a wardrobe and occasions planner — and offline games (Sudoku and detective mysteries). The stories, games and tools are bundled inside the App, so reading, writing and playing all work without an internet connection.

The App connects to the internet only for a small number of services run by Google LLC: showing ads, fetching remote configuration, reporting crashes, counting usage, delivering general notifications, verifying that the App is a genuine copy, and loading the icons of our other apps when you open that section. Those services are described below. Without a connection the App keeps working and simply shows no ads.

Prayer times are calculated on your device from the city you choose in a built-in list, and the Qibla direction is worked out from that city and your phone’s compass sensor, also on the device. The App never reads your location.

4. Health information

The period and pregnancy sections let you record cycle dates, symptoms and notes, your pregnancy dates, weekly notes, baby-kick and contraction sessions, test results, follow-up appointments and pregnancy photos.

  • These records are stored only on your device. They are never transmitted to us, to Google or to anyone else.
  • They are never used for advertising or analytics, never sold and never shared.
  • They are excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.
  • They leave the device only if you create a backup file and choose where to save it (see section 14).
  • You can delete them at any time from inside each section, or by clearing the App’s storage.

The App is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition. Cycle, ovulation and due-date predictions are mathematical estimates for information only. They are not a diagnosis, not medical advice and not a method of contraception. See the Terms of Use.

5. Information we do not collect

  • No name, e-mail address, phone number, password or any other account data — the App has no accounts.
  • No location: no GPS and no network location. You pick your city by hand for prayer times.
  • No contacts, SMS, call logs, calendar or microphone.
  • No access to your photo library: you pick one photo at a time through the Android photo picker, or take a photo with your own camera app, and only that photo is copied into the App.
  • No fingerprint or face data: fingerprint unlock is verified by Android itself, and the App receives only “success” or “failure”.
  • No payment details. The App sells nothing.
  • No reading of your notes, health records, photos or anything else you write — none of it is uploaded.

10. Advertising

The App is free and funded by ads served by Google AdMob. You may see:

  • Banner ads fixed at the bottom of many screens, always separate from the App’s own content.
  • Interstitial (full-screen) ads at natural breaks — for example when opening a story, moving to the next part, or going to the next Sudoku level or detective case. They are limited in frequency.
  • Rewarded ads that you choose to watch to get a hint in Sudoku or in the detective game. The offer is explained before the ad plays, hints are limited per period, they are never needed to finish a game, and nothing is ever charged.
  • App-open ads when the App starts or returns from the background — never on the very first launch, and by default at most about once an hour.

To serve and measure ads, AdMob and its partners may use your device’s Advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, and information about your device and the ads you interact with. Personalised ads are shown only where allowed and, in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, only if you consent.

11. Analytics and crash reports

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand, in aggregate, how the App is used, and Firebase Crashlytics to detect and fix crashes. The App records only the events these services collect automatically — it logs no custom events, sets no user ID and never sends the content of your stories, notes, records or photos. Analytics may read the Advertising ID to measure usage across sessions; you can reset or delete it as described in section 10. Crash reports may include the state of the App at the moment of the crash and basic integrity flags, but never your personal content.

12. Notifications

  • Reminders you set — the adhan, reading, habits, diary, schedule, period, pregnancy appointments and wardrobe occasions — are created and scheduled on your device by the App itself. They involve no server.
  • General notifications about new stories and news are delivered through Firebase Cloud Messaging using a notification token. Turning off «جديد التطبيق» or all notifications in the App’s settings deletes the token.

On Android 13 and later notifications are opt-in, and you can block them for the App at any time in Android Settings.

13. Security and app integrity

  • Diary notes, pregnancy follow-up appointments and favourite stories are kept in an encrypted database whose key is protected by the Android Keystore. Everything else is kept in the App’s private storage, which other apps cannot read.
  • You can lock the diary, the tracking sections and the schedule with a password, a pattern or your fingerprint. Lock codes are stored as a salted one-way hash, so the code itself cannot be recovered from the device.
  • All communication with Google services uses encrypted HTTPS connections. The App allows no unencrypted traffic.
  • Before ads are served, the App asks the Google Play Integrity API (through Firebase App Check) to confirm that it is an unmodified copy installed from Google Play on a genuine device. The result contains no personal information. A copy that has been modified or re-signed is also detected by the App itself and will not run.
  • No security measure is perfect. If you believe your device has been compromised, uninstall the App and reinstall it from Google Play.

14. Backups and exported files

Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are turned off for all of the App’s data, so nothing from the App is copied to your Google account by the system.

If you choose «تصدير نسخة احتياطية» (export a backup), the App creates one file containing all your data — including health records and photos — and saves it to the place you choose, such as your phone’s storage or a cloud drive you select. This file is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere safe; any service you save it to handles it under its own policy. Restoring a backup replaces the App’s current data on the device with the data in the file.

16. Deleting your data

Because the App has no accounts, you are in direct control of your data:

  • Inside the App: each section lets you delete its entries, such as a note, a habit, a cycle record or a wardrobe piece.
  • Clearing storage or uninstalling (Android Settings → Apps → روايات قصص → Storage → Clear storage) deletes everything the App keeps on your device and invalidates the Firebase installation ID and the notification token.
  • Exported backup files are deleted like any other file, from the place where you saved them.
  • Advertising data is disconnected from you when you choose “Delete advertising ID” in Android Settings.
  • Data held by Google services is identified only by random identifiers that we cannot link to a person. If you nevertheless want us to request its erasure, e-mail userconplay@gmail.com; we will act within 30 days and tell you what could be done.

Step-by-step instructions are on the Data & Permissions page.
