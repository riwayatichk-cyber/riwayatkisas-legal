This page explains how to delete the data of Riwayat Kisas – Offline Novels & Stories (com.booklivre.kolobtajaraaathob), how to ask us to request erasure of data held by Google services, and which permissions the App uses and why.

  • There is no account to delete. The App has no sign-up and no log-in.
  • We keep no copy of your content. Your notes, records, photos and progress live only on your phone, so deleting them there deletes them completely.
  • Google services hold only technical data linked to random identifiers, described in the Privacy Policy.

2. Delete everything on your device

  1. If you want to keep a copy, first export a backup from the App’s settings and save it somewhere safe.
  2. Open Android Settings → Apps → روايات قصص → Storage.
  3. Tap Clear storage (sometimes called “Clear data”) and confirm. Every note, record, photo, setting and progress item kept by the App is deleted, and the Firebase installation ID and notification token become invalid.
  4. Alternatively, uninstall the App. Android deletes all of its data together with the App.
This cannot be undone. We have no copy of your data and cannot restore it.

3. Exported backup files

Backup files you exported are ordinary files saved where you chose — on your phone or in a cloud drive you selected. Clearing or uninstalling the App does not delete them. Delete them like any other file, from your file manager or from that cloud drive. Remember that backup files are not encrypted and include your health records and photos.

4. Data held by Google services

  • Advertising ID: open Android Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads) and choose Delete advertising ID, or Reset advertising ID to get a new one. Ad data linked to the old ID is then disconnected from your device.
  • Personalised ads: change your choice at any time in the App under Settings → «إعدادات موافقة الإعلانات».
  • Notification token: turn off «جديد التطبيق», or all notifications, in the App’s settings. The token is deleted from Firebase Cloud Messaging.
  • Analytics and crash data: linked only to random installation identifiers that change when you reinstall the App. Crash reports are deleted after 90 days and analytics events within 14 months.

5. Ask us by e-mail

If you want us to request erasure of the technical data that Google services hold for your installation, or to delete a message you sent us:

  1. E-mail userconplay@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion request – Riwayat Kisas”.
  2. Say what you want deleted. If you can, include your Advertising ID (Android Settings → Google → Ads), because it is the only identifier that can locate advertising data.
  3. We reply within 30 days, act on the request, and tell you what was deleted and what is handled directly by Google under its own retention rules.

6. What remains after deletion

  • Nothing of your personal content remains anywhere we control — we never had a copy.
  • Aggregated statistics that no longer identify any installation may be kept.
  • Google may keep some technical or advertising data under its own retention rules, described in the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Backup files you exported remain until you delete them.

How to revoke a permission

  1. Open Android Settings → Apps → روايات قصص.
  2. Tap Permissions and switch off what you want, or tap Notifications to block them.
  3. For exact alarms, open Alarms & reminders (Android 12 and later) and turn the switch off.
