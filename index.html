The short version

  • No account, no sign-up. The App never asks for your name, e-mail address or phone number, and we run no servers that store your content.
  • Everything you write stays on your phone. Diary notes, schedules, habits, prayer log, wardrobe and game progress are kept only in the App’s private storage, with the most personal items in an encrypted database.
  • Health records never leave your device. Period and pregnancy data are never sent anywhere, never used for ads or analytics, and are excluded from Android cloud backup.
  • No location, camera, microphone or contacts permission. You choose your city by hand for prayer times, and photos come only from the Android photo picker or your own camera app.
  • The App is free and supported by ads served by Google AdMob, with a consent form where the law requires one. There is nothing to buy.
  • Google services receive technical data only: crash reports, usage statistics, a push-notification token, an app-integrity check and the device’s Advertising ID.
  • Deleting the App deletes its data from your device. Backup files you exported stay where you saved them until you delete them.
